Runway coach

A Python auditor checks the note. If the coach mentions a smile, a weight, or a number the camera did not measure, the note is replaced.

Staff

Who speaks, and who only drafts.

  1. You pick the room. On-stage, news, or runway. The app does not spend a model call to decide that.
  2. One coach drafts, and only with the tools in that room: the question book and the clock, the wire packet, or the measurements.
  3. The director releases interview and news. A second model call. It can tighten a sentence. It cannot invent a score or a headline.
  4. The runway auditor is not a model. Invented measurements are a math problem. A checker is cheaper and stricter than another prose pass.
Contestant → Coach + tools → Director or auditor → The note
