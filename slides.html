Call Time

A rehearsal room for pageant contestants 18 and older who cannot hire a coach. It times the answer, grades tonight’s news against the headline, and coaches the walk from numbers that never leave the browser.

https://github.com/rgray601/call-time

Miss Wisconsin USA 2018 · Miss Supranational USA 2019 · gpt-6-luna only

The hour she cannot buy

  • An individual interview session is listed at $125 for 55 minutes.
  • A walk session is listed at $100–$125. Interview coaching at another studio is $75 an hour.
  • An online interview room is $297 a year, and it takes teen contestants.
  • Call Time is the reps: the clock, the wire, the line of the walk. Adults only. No body coaching.

Prices checked 23 November 2026. Pageant Winner Consulting, Haute Couture Agency, Center Stage Elite, Pageant Success Interview Room.

Three rooms. She picks.

  • On-stage. 25, 30, or 90 seconds. The coach calls the question, the clock, and the filler count.
  • News desk. BBC and NPR only. The model cannot open a link she pastes, and it cannot invent a story when the wires are down.
  • Runway. Twelve seconds. The video stays in the browser. The coach may talk about the measurements, and a checker replaces the note if it doesn’t.

No router model. Drawing a question, loading headlines, and previewing a walk cost $0.

Who is allowed to speak

  • Python screens the words, then the budget, before any model.
  • Interview coach drafts. Director releases. The director cannot rescore.
  • News desk looks up ids from the packet. Unknown ids are dropped. Director releases.
  • Runway coach reads the metrics. A second model does not audit the note. Python does.

All three models are gpt-6-luna. Reasoning is off, because luna rejects tools when it is on. final_result in a trace is pydantic-ai’s structured output.

The $5 daily cap holds about 980 heavy nights, then stops. The $611 figure is the model bill if the work is allowed to finish.

The locks fired at $0

  • Prompt injection: blocked, no model.
  • Diet request: blocked as body coaching.
  • A pretended $0.75 wallet trips the same budget lock. Charged $0.
